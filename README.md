## What is GrishteSync?

GrishteSync is an AI‑powered coding agent that takes a simple text prompt, autonomously creates a branch, writes the code and tests, bumps your version, and opens a polished Pull Request — all synced perfectly with your workflow.  

It's the season’s first rain for your codebase: after the heat of an idea comes the shower of a complete, tested, and versioned release.

---

## Why the name?

- **Grishma** – the intense summer heat (your prompt’s creative fire).  
- **Sync** – harmony, automation, and seamless integration.  

Together they capture the magic of turning a desire into a shipped artifact — effortlessly and in perfect rhythm.

---

## Core features

- 🌿 **Agentbranch workflow** – works in an isolated branch, never touches main until you approve.  
- 🧠 **AI‑generated code & tests** – powered by Groq (free tier), soon DeepSeek.  
- 📦 **Automatic version bumping** – semantic, clean, no manual hassle.  
- 📝 **Instant PR description** – beautifully formatted, ready to review.  
- ✅ **Approve or Re‑do** – you stay in full control.

---

## How it works

1. You type a prompt (e.g., *“Add dark mode toggle with unit tests”*).  
2. GrishteSync spins up an agentbranch, generates the changes and tests, bumps the version, and commits.  
3. You get a Pull Request link with a full description.  
4. Approve to merge, or re‑do until it’s perfect.
